About me

Compassionate support rooted in learning and care.

I am Samson Alosi Obongo, a Level 3 student in Clinical Psychology at Year 3. I am building my professional foundation in psychological understanding, emotional wellbeing, and evidence-based support for individuals facing life challenges.

My work is guided by empathy, active listening, and a strong interest in helping people understand their emotions, strengthen resilience, and improve mental health. I am especially motivated by issues involving stress, anxiety, self-development, and personal healing.

  • CBT, mindfulness-based therapy, and trauma-informed care
  • Warm, confidential, and non-judgmental sessions
  • Virtual and in-person appointments available
  • Research-based interventions
