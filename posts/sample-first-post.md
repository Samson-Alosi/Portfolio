Anxiety is common and treatable. In this short post I'll outline three practical steps you can try this week:

1. Notice the physical signs. Pause and name what you feel — e.g., racing heart, tight chest, shallow breath.
2. Use a 3-3-3 grounding exercise: name 3 things you can see, 3 sounds you can hear, and move 3 parts of your body.
3. Practice a single 60-second breathing exercise: inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 6s.
