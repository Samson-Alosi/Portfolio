Stress can affect people differently. You might notice tension, busy thoughts, a long to-do list, or a wish to be around someone you trust. Noticing what is happening can help you choose a small next step without judging yourself.

This short check-in is for reflection only. It is **not a validated screening questionnaire**, does not calculate a score, and cannot diagnose a mental-health condition. Your choices stay in this browser; the check-in does not save or send them.

## Two-minute stress check-in

Take a moment and select any statements that fit how things feel right now. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can skip any question.

### Choose what feels manageable

You do not need to try every suggestion. Choose one that feels safe and realistic for you:

- **If your body feels tense:** Notice your feet on the floor and look around, naming a few things you see or hear. Let your breathing happen naturally; stop if focusing on it feels uncomfortable.
- **If thoughts feel busy:** Gently name what you notice, such as “I’m noticing a worry,” then bring your attention back to the next small thing you choose to do.
- **If demands feel overwhelming:** Write down one small next action. You do not need to solve everything at once.
- **If you feel disconnected:** Consider sending a short message to someone you trust, such as “Could we talk or check in today?”

These are optional coping ideas, not treatment instructions. A strategy may not suit everyone; stop and choose another approach if it makes you feel worse.

### When to seek support

Consider speaking with a qualified health or mental-health professional if distress persists, worsens, or makes everyday life difficult. You can also explore [ways to recognize anxiety](post.html?post=anxiety-signs) or [small grounding rituals](post.html?post=nervous-system-reset) from this blog.

If you are in immediate danger or cannot stay safe, contact local emergency services. In Rwanda, the website lists emergency number **112** and mental-health support number **116**. This check-in is not monitored and is not an emergency service.

If you would like to discuss support options, you can [book a session](https://calendly.com/samclinicalpsychologist/clinical-consultation).
