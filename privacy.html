Privacy information

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice explains how information submitted through this website's contact form is handled. It does not describe clinical records or replace a separate privacy notice for any professional service.

What the form collects

The form asks for your first and last name, email address, the type of support you are interested in, and a message. The website also sends a submission timestamp so an inquiry can be identified and answered.

How an inquiry is handled

The form first attempts to save the inquiry to Supabase. If that request is unavailable or returns an error, the website submits the form to Formspree as a fallback. These providers process information under their own privacy terms: Supabase Privacy Policy and Formspree Privacy Policy.

The information is intended to let Samson respond to your inquiry. Do not assume that messages submitted here are encrypted end-to-end or covered by clinical confidentiality.

Storage, access, and retention

Supabase stores submissions in the website's inquiries table. The current database policy allows submissions from the public and permits reads by authenticated Supabase users; it is not restricted to a named site-owner account. Formspree may also receive submissions when Supabase is unavailable or reports an error.

No automatic deletion schedule is currently configured for these inquiries. The providers may retain submitted information according to their own terms and policies. You can email Sam.clinicalpsychology@gmail.com with questions or to request deletion; provider-held copies are also subject to the providers' processes.

Please keep messages general

Do not include sensitive personal information, detailed health or trauma history, passwords, or other confidential details in the contact form. A brief description of the kind of appointment you are seeking is enough. This form is not monitored for emergencies. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
